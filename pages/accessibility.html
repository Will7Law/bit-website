Accessibility
Statement

Our commitment to making BIT's digital services accessible to everyone, regardless of ability or device.

Last updated: January 2026

The Board of Industrial Training is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all users, including persons with disabilities.

Our Commitment

We strive to comply with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. Our goal is to ensure that all citizens, regardless of ability, can access information about our programmes, submit applications, verify certificates, and engage with BIT online.

Accessibility Features

This website includes the following accessibility features:

  • Semantic HTML: Proper heading hierarchy, landmarks, and ARIA labels for screen reader compatibility
  • Keyboard Navigation: All interactive elements are accessible via keyboard (Tab, Enter, Escape)
  • Alternative Text: Descriptive alt text is provided for all meaningful images
  • Colour Contrast: Text meets WCAG 2.1 AA minimum contrast ratios (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text)
  • Responsive Design: The site is fully functional across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
  • Resizable Text: All text can be resized up to 200% without loss of content or functionality
  • Form Labels: All form inputs have associated labels and clear error messages
  • Skip Navigation: Skip-to-content functionality for keyboard users
  • Focus Indicators: Visible focus outlines on all interactive elements

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some limitations may exist:

  • Some older PDF documents may not be fully accessible; we are working to remediate these
  • Third-party embedded content (such as maps) may have their own accessibility limitations

Feedback & Assistance

If you encounter any accessibility barriers on this website, or if you need information in an alternative format, please contact us:

Continuous Improvement

We regularly review and test our website for accessibility compliance and are committed to ongoing improvements. This statement was last reviewed on April 1, 2026.
