Programme Benefits

What You Get With BIT Certification

Hands-On Training

80%+ practical instruction in real workshop environments with industry-standard tools and equipment.

Certified Credentials

Nationally recognised BIT certificate aligned with Caribbean TVET standards and frameworks.

Industry Connections

Direct pathways to employment through partnerships with GNIC, GuySuCo, BOSAI, and more.

Affordable Access

Government-subsidised fees ensure quality vocational training is accessible to all Guyanese citizens.
