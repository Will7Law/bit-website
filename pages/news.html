March 28, 2026 | Infrastructure

New $55.4 Million Region 6 Training Facility Officially Commissioned

The Board of Industrial Training has officially opened its newest state-of-the-art training facility in Berbice, Region 6. The $55.4 million investment significantly expands BIT's capacity for trades training in the Berbice corridor, featuring modern workshops equipped with industry-standard tools and equipment for welding, electrical, carpentry, and IT programmes.
