Common Questions

Verification FAQs

Who can verify a BIT certificate?
Anyone holding a certificate code may request verification. The service is designed primarily for employers, educational institutions and third parties who need to confirm the authenticity of a candidate’s BIT credentials. Requests are handled by our Certification Department, who check the code against BIT’s official register.
Where do I find the certificate code?
The unique certificate code is printed on the bottom-right corner of every BIT certificate. It follows the format BIT-YYYY-XX-NNNNN (e.g., BIT-2025-EL-04821), where YYYY is the year, XX is the programme code, and NNNNN is the serial number.
How long does verification take?
Our Certification Department normally responds within two business days. Verification is carried out manually against BIT’s official register. If your request is urgent, please call +592 225-1077 and ask for the Certification Department. An instant online lookup is currently in development and will be announced on this page when available.
Is this service free?
Yes. Certificate verification through the BIT website is completely free of charge for all users.

Need Help With Verification?

Contact our Certification Department for assistance with certificate verification or collection.
