Policy

How the Board of Industrial Training collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: January 2026

Last updated: April 1, 2026

The Board of Industrial Training ("BIT", "we", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of visitors to our website (bit.gov.gy) and users of our online services, including the Trainee Application Portal and Certificate Verification Portal.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, date of birth, national ID number, email address, phone number, home address, and photograph — collected through our application portal.
  • Educational Information: Academic qualifications, prior training records, and work experience — collected as part of programme applications.
  • Contact Form Data: Name, email, phone number, and message content submitted through our contact form.
  • Website Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and session duration — collected through analytics tools for site improvement purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

Personal data collected by BIT is used exclusively for:

  • Processing and evaluating trainee applications
  • Communicating with applicants regarding their application status
  • Issuing and verifying certificates through our Certificate Verification Portal
  • Responding to enquiries submitted through our contact form
  • Improving website functionality and user experience through anonymised analytics
  • Fulfilling our legal and regulatory obligations under the Industrial Training Act, Chapter 39:04

3. Data Storage & Security

All personal data is stored on secure servers with encryption at rest and in transit (SSL/TLS). Access to personal data is restricted to authorised BIT staff who require it for their duties. We implement industry-standard security measures including firewalls, malware scanning, and regular security audits.

4. Data Sharing

BIT does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. Data may be shared with:

  • The Ministry of Labour & Manpower Planning (our supervising ministry)
  • Government agencies as required by law
  • Certificate verification requestors (limited to name, programme, date, and centre only)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies for essential functionality and anonymised analytics. No tracking cookies from third-party advertisers are used. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Contact Us

For questions about this privacy policy or to exercise your data rights, contact:
