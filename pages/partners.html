National Partners

Building Guyana Together

GNIC
National Partner

Guyana National Industrial Corporation

GNIC partners with BIT for apprenticeship co-placements, providing trainees with real-world industrial experience. Through this collaboration, BIT students gain direct exposure to manufacturing and industrial environments, enhancing their practical skills and employment readiness.

GuySuCo
National Partner

Guyana Sugar Corporation

GuySuCo and BIT maintain a longstanding training partnership focused on estate-based vocational education. This collaboration provides specialised trades training for workers in the sugar industry, supporting both community development and industrial productivity.

BOSAI
Industry Partner

BOSAI Minerals

BOSAI Minerals collaborates with BIT on bauxite sector training and certification programmes. This partnership ensures that mining sector workers receive standardised, industry-relevant skills training aligned with international safety and operational standards.

DHB
National Partner

Demerara Harbour Bridge Corporation

The DHB Corporation partners with BIT for equipment and trades training programmes, providing trainees with practical exposure to heavy equipment operations, bridge maintenance, and infrastructure construction techniques.

International Partners

Global Collaboration

ILO
International Standards

International Labour Organization

The ILO provides standards alignment and curriculum support, ensuring BIT's programmes meet international benchmarks for vocational education. This partnership enhances the global recognition and portability of BIT certifications.

TVET
Regional Standards

Caribbean TVET Council

The TVET Council provides the regional standards and certification framework within which BIT operates. This partnership ensures BIT certifications are recognised across Caribbean nations, facilitating graduate mobility and regional employment opportunities.
